## My Favorite Series: Alice in Borderland

**Alice in Borderland**  is a Japanese science fiction and survival thriller series based on the manga written by Haro Aso. It follows a group of people who are transported to an abandoned version of Tokyo and forced to participate in dangerous games to survive.

### Series Information
- **Title:** Alice in Borderland
- **Genre:** Science Fiction, Thriller, Survival, Mystery
- **Based on:** Manga by Haro Aso
- **Country:** Japan
- **Platform:** Netflix

### Why I Like This Series
I like this series because of its exciting challenges, unpredictable plot twists, and interesting characters. Every game requires intelligence, teamwork, strategy, and courage. It also makes me curious about what will happen next in the story.

### My Favorite Character
Arisu – I like his intelligence and ability to solve difficult problems, especially when facing challenging survival games.

### My Favorite Elements
1. The challenging survival games
2. The unexpected plot twists
3. The characters' intelligence and teamwork
4. The mystery behind the Borderland

### Rating
**My Rating: 10/10**

**Survival is not just about strength, but also intelligence, courage, and determination.**
